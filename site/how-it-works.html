How it works

Straight from one device to the other

No cloud in between: the phone and the Mac find each other, check each other’s certificate and pass the clip directly.

The phone and the Mac pair once with a QR code that carries the Mac’s certificate fingerprint and a one-time token. Both screens show the same 6-digit code, so you can confirm it’s really your Mac. From then on they talk only to each other over TLS 1.3, and each side checks the other’s certificate.

Over Tailscale, traffic may pass through Tailscale’s relays, still encrypted end to end between your devices.

Everything you copy is synced, passwords and 2FA codes included. Nothing is filtered or held back.

Encryption
TLS 1.3, mutual authentication, certificates pinned by SHA-256
Network
Same Wi-Fi via Bonjour _syncliko._tcp on port 47823, or Tailscale by MagicDNS name or 100.x address
Latency
0.1–0.4 s from copy to the other clipboard on Wi-Fi, measured on a Galaxy Z Fold 8
Battery
The connection sleeps 30 s after the screen turns off and reconnects in about 0.2 s when it turns on. Anything you copied on the Mac in the last 2 minutes is waiting.
Data
Text, links and images (PNG, JPEG, HEIC) up to 20 MB, in 256 KB chunks
Servers
None. No accounts, no analytics.
Requires
Android 12+ (phones, tablets, foldables) and macOS 26+ on Apple silicon
