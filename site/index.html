Both ways

Works both ways, in the apps you already use

Copy on the phone, paste on the Mac, or the reverse. No more sending links to yourself.

  • Android to MacA link from a chatSelect it, tap Copy, then ⌘V on the Mac.
  • Mac to AndroidAn address from a website⌘C on the Mac, then Paste in the ride or maps app.
  • Android to MacA photo or a screenshotCopy it in Gallery or Share → Syncliko, then paste into Slack, Figma or Mail. Up to 20 MB.
  • iPhone to Mac to AndroidText from a work iPhoneUniversal Clipboard puts it on the Mac, and Syncliko passes it on to the phone.

Android → Mac

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Does it work with an iPhone or Windows?

No. Syncliko connects an Android phone to a Mac. An iPhone and a Mac already share a clipboard through Apple’s Universal Clipboard, and Syncliko passes those copies on to your Android phone.

Why isn’t it on Google Play or the App Store?

Automatic sending relies on an accessibility service and, optionally, log access granted over ADB. Store rules don’t allow that for a clipboard app, so you install Syncliko directly: an APK on the phone and an app you drag to Applications on the Mac.

Why does Android say “pasted from your clipboard”?

Android 12 and later show that message whenever an app reads the clipboard. Apps can’t hide it. You’ll see it each time Syncliko picks up something you copied.

Do both devices need to be on the same network?

On the same Wi-Fi they find each other automatically. Guest networks that keep devices apart won’t work. Away from home, install Tailscale on both and Syncliko connects through it.

Will it drain the battery?

No. While the screen is off there’s no connection at all. On a Galaxy Z Fold 8 it used under 0.1 mAh over four hours of standby.

What happens to passwords and 2FA codes?

They sync like anything else, so a code from your authenticator or an SMS lands on the Mac the moment you copy it. Copies a password manager marks as secret keep that mark on the other device. Like every clip, they travel only between your own paired devices, encrypted.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall the app on each device; its pairing data goes with it. On the Mac you can also delete the “com.syncliko.identity” item in Keychain Access.

Install

Install on both, pair with a QR code

Mac

  1. Open the disk image and drag Syncliko to Applications.
  2. Open Syncliko from Applications and confirm when macOS asks about an app downloaded from the internet. It’s notarized by Apple.
  3. Click the Syncliko icon in the menu bar, then Add Device to show a QR code.
  4. In Privacy & Security → Paste from Other Apps, set Syncliko to Always Allow.

Android

  1. Download the APK and open it. Allow installs from your browser when Android asks.
  2. Scan the QR code on your Mac and check that both screens show the same 6-digit code.
  3. Allow local network access, notifications and unrestricted battery use.
  4. Turn on Standard in Accessibility settings. If it’s greyed out, open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings first. Or set up Pro over ADB.
